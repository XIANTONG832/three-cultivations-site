## Description

Three Cultivations is a quiet daily practice for moral character, intellectual clarity, and aesthetic attention.

Inspired by Cai Yuanpei's vision of education for a complete person, the app turns big ideas into small actions you can try in ordinary life. It is not a quote feed and it is not a leaderboard. It is a private place to notice what you do, how you think, and what you are able to see.

### Three ways to grow

Moral

Practice self-cultivation, responsibility, public spirit, and the choices that shape character.

Intellectual

Slow down your conclusions. Practice independent judgment, evidence, curiosity, and clear thinking.

Aesthetic

Return to the ordinary world with attention: light, sound, proportion, order, and creation.

### A simple weekly rhythm

• Choose a weekly theme from the local library.
• Try one small practice in a real situation.
• Mark Done, A lapse, or Not encountered.
• End the day with one honest sentence.
• Review the week without turning your character into a score.

Three Cultivations is designed to be calm, private, and useful. Your themes, practice records, and reflections stay on your device in the current version.

Begin with one small practice. Let the record become a way of seeing.
