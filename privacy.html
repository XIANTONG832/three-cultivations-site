Privacy Policy

Three Cultivations is designed as a private, local-first reflection tool. This policy explains what the app stores and what it does not collect.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your selected theme, daily practice records, and reflection notes locally on your device. This information is used only to display your cultivation history and personalize the local experience.

No account and no server collection

The current version does not require an account, sign-in, or server connection. We do not receive, store, or access your personal reflections.

No tracking or advertising

The current version does not include advertising, analytics SDKs, third-party tracking, or data sharing with third parties.

How to delete your data

Your records can be removed by deleting the app from your device. Because the current version does not maintain a server copy, deleting the app removes the local app data.

Children's privacy

Three Cultivations does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated before those changes take effect.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please use the support contact provided on the app's App Store listing.
