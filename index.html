Three dimensions of growth

A broader idea of self-improvement.

M

Moral

Practice responsibility, self-cultivation, public spirit, and the small choices that shape character.

I

Intellectual

Slow down your conclusions. Welcome evidence, independent judgment, curiosity, and revision.

A

Aesthetic

Return to the ordinary world with attention: light, sound, proportion, order, and creation.

A simple weekly rhythm

Less pressure. More awareness.

01

Choose a theme

Begin with a locally stored theme inspired by Cai Yuanpei's vision of moral, intellectual, and aesthetic education.

02

Try one small practice

Meet the theme in a real situation: a conversation, a claim, a familiar street, or a quiet decision.

03

Leave a truthful record

Mark done, a lapse, or not encountered. One honest sentence is enough for the evening reflection.

“A small practice is enough for today.”
